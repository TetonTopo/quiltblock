How a custom block gets made

  1. Describe it“A Highland cow, side profile, on skis.” Or upload a photo.
  2. SimplifyColours collapse to a handful of fabrics. Fur becomes big flat shapes.
  3. Snap to the gridShapes become squares, triangles, hourglasses and flying geese.
  4. Check the rulesNo curves, nothing too small to sew, piece count kept sane. Then the PDF.
