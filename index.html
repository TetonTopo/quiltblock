How a custom block gets made

  1. Describe it“A teapot with a curly spout.” Or upload a photo. Say how hard you want it.
  2. SimplifyColours collapse to a handful of fabrics. Curves become corners.
  3. Snap to the gridShapes become squares, stitch-and-flip corners, triangles, hourglasses and flying geese.
  4. Check the rulesNo curves, nothing too small to sew, piece count inside your level. Then the pattern.
